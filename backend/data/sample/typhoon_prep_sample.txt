Go-Bag Essentials
Assemble a go-bag for each household member. Include at least three liters of
drinking water per person per day for three days, non-perishable food, a manual
can opener, a first-aid kit, a flashlight with extra batteries, a battery or
hand-crank radio, copies of important documents in a waterproof bag, cash in
small bills, and a power bank for your phone.

Evacuation Basics
Know your nearest designated evacuation center and at least two routes to reach
it. If local authorities issue an evacuation order, leave immediately and do not
wait for conditions to worsen. Do not walk or drive through floodwater; even
shallow moving water can knock a person down and a vehicle can be swept away.

During a Power Outage
Keep refrigerator doors closed to preserve food. Use flashlights instead of
candles to reduce fire risk. Unplug sensitive electronics to avoid damage from
power surges when electricity returns. Check on elderly or disabled neighbors.

After the Storm
Avoid downed or dangling power lines and report them to authorities. Discard
food that contacted floodwater. Boil or treat drinking water until officials
confirm the supply is safe. Beware of standing water that can carry disease.
